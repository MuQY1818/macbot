01 / workflow

    
One workflow, from teleoperation to a running policy.

    
Datasets and checkpoints stay in the LeRobot format. They move between the MacBook and your GPU machine as they are.

  

  

    
macbot init
Pick the arm and cameras and check your saved LeRobot calibration. Already have a LeRobot command? Import it with --from-lerobot.

    
macbot collect
Record with live camera previews and a check of every episode. No arm yet? Try collect --fake.

    
macbot export
Write a LeRobot v3.0 dataset. health inspects local or Hub datasets in place.

    
lerobot-train
Train with the standard LeRobot trainer on any GPU machine, then bring the checkpoint back.

    
macbot run
Load a local or Hub checkpoint and record every trial with cameras and timing.

    
macbot replay
Align trial video, joint traces and action chunks on one timeline.

04 / macOS

    
What it took to run this on macOS.

  

  

    
CAN under sudo
macbot preloads PCBUSB and gives recorded files back to the invoking user.

    
Camera selection
AVFoundation unique IDs keep UVC camera roles separate from iPhone Continuity Camera.

    
VLA inference
A compiled PyTorch MPS path, plus an optional MLX backend for π0.5, π0 and SmolVLA.

    
Safety
Every console starts paused or idle, limits joint speed and range, clamps the gripper force, pauses when a camera stalls and returns to zero slowly before disabling the motors.
